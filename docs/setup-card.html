Your Tobia Talk box

Talk to the family with one button. It works anywhere with Wi-Fi. Nothing to install on your phone.

Set it up (once, about 3 minutes)

1

Plug it in with the USB-C cable and any phone charger. The screen says Join WiFi: TobiaTalk.

2

On your phone, open Wi-Fi settings and join the network called TobiaTalk (no password). A setup page opens by itself. If it doesn't, open your web browser and go to 192.168.4.1.

3

Fill it in: your name, the picture you want to send, your home Wi-Fi and its password. Then press Save. The box restarts and joins your Wi-Fi.

4

Done! The screen shows your name and "Hold to talk".
Your home Wi-Fi must be the normal 2.4 GHz kind (most are). Wi-Fi that asks you to sign in on a web page (hotels, schools) won't work.

Using it

The box has two buttons: BOOT (the one the arrow on the screen points to) and PWR.

Hold BOOT and talkLet go to send. Between 5 and 15 seconds.
Tap BOOTSends your picture (see below).
New messageThe light blinks and the screen shows who it is from. Tap PWR to hear it.
Tap PWR (nothing new)Shows the last 10 messages. PWR goes older, BOOT goes newer, hold PWR for 3 seconds to hear one again. Both buttons together to leave.
Screen went to the heart?That is the screensaver. Tap any button to wake it.

New messages can take up to a minute to arrive.

Change your picture

Tap PWR three times fast. The six pictures appear. Press PWR to move the frame, BOOT to keep the one you like.

New Wi-Fi, or it says "No WiFi"

Hold PWR for 5 seconds until the screen says Join WiFi, then do steps 2 and 3 again. If your Wi-Fi changed, un-tick "Keep my current Wi-Fi" and pick the new one.

Tip: keep it plugged in. The battery is only for carrying it around and lasts a few hours. To switch it off on battery, hold both buttons for 10 seconds.
